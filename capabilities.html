Capability map

What Bowyer Intelligence builds.

Eight capability areas, one discipline: systems that carry real operational load, with controls and audit trails around them. If a machine should be doing it, we make the machine, and make it governable.

01 · Intelligent automation

Workflow automation, unattended processing, reporting automation, process automation, integrations, operational tooling, rule-based systems and document workflows. The hallmark: work that used to wait for a person now runs on schedule, with failures routed to people instead of blocking the pipeline.

  • workflow automation
  • unattended processing
  • reporting automation
  • integrations
  • rule-based systems
  • document workflows

02 · Applied AI

Generative AI where it earns its keep: LLM systems, RAG knowledge assistants over your own documentation, AI-enabled internal tools, VLM/computer-vision validation, AI-assisted review of model outputs, and agentic workflows with human checkpoints. The test is never the demo: it is whether the system still behaves inside a real operation.

  • LLM systems
  • RAG assistants
  • VLM / computer vision
  • AI validation
  • agentic workflows

03 · Data intelligence

Analytics, business intelligence, dashboards, forecasting, anomaly detection, executive decision support, strategic and operational analytics, and the data pipelines that feed them. Built for decisions first: every number traceable to its source, every dashboard answerable to the operation it measures.

  • analytics & BI
  • forecasting
  • anomaly detection
  • decision support
  • data pipelines

04 · Software

Custom applications, websites, web applications, bespoke internal systems, dashboards, workflow systems, utilities and integrations. Right-sized engineering: no framework fashion, no unnecessary backend, just dependable software shaped around the way your operation actually runs.

  • custom applications
  • web applications
  • internal systems
  • workflow systems
  • integrations

05 · Financial automation

Statement processing, financial-document extraction, transaction classification, reconciliation assistance, financial reporting pipelines, exception identification and management reporting. Designed with financial/audit discipline: classifications are evidence-linked, exceptions surface rather than silently resolve, and humans approve what matters.

  • statement processing
  • transaction classification
  • reconciliation assistance
  • exception identification

06 · Document & statement automation

Raw document ingestion, extraction, classification, validation, structured output and review workflows. Documents in any state (scans, PDFs, spreadsheets) flow through a governed pipeline and emerge as structured, validated, decision-ready data with provenance intact.

  • ingestion
  • extraction
  • classification
  • validation
  • review workflows

07 · FICA & compliance support

Document collection, completeness checking, workflow tracking, evidence organisation, structured review, exception handling, audit trails and governance tooling for compliance-support operations such as FICA document workflows. Important: this is software that supports your compliance process; it does not constitute legal advice, does not guarantee compliance with FICA or any regulation, and replaces nothing your compliance officer, legal adviser or regulator requires of you.

  • document collection
  • completeness checking
  • evidence organisation
  • audit trails
  • compliance-SUPPORT only

08 · Governance & operational systems

Controls, traceability, validation, auditability, evidence, process visibility, QA and structured operational execution. Automation without governance is a liability; every system we build records what ran, when, on what inputs, with what outcome, so automated work can be trusted, audited and improved.

  • controls
  • traceability
  • auditability
  • process visibility
  • structured execution

How an engagement works

From painful process to governed pipeline.

MapThe workflow is observed as it actually happens: inputs, hand-offs, failure points, controls.
DesignThe pipeline is specified: steps, validation rules, exception routes, audit points.
BuildAutomation, AI and software components are built and integrated, right-sized, no ceremony.
ProveThe system is validated against real operational scenarios, including adversarial inputs.
Hand overUnattended operation with monitoring, documentation and owner-level control.
