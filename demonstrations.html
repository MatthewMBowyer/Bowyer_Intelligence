Demonstration 01

Manual workflow → automated compliance-support pipeline

A FICA-style document pack arrives in twelve formats from twelve people. Manually, that is a checklist someone grinds through. Here, watch the same intake run as a governed pipeline: extraction, verification, routing and an audit trail, all running here.

compliance-intake-pipeline (synthetic run)
Intakefiles land, checksummed

What you are actually seeing

  • The stages transition live: idle → running (pulsing node) → done (green), driven by the same state-machine pattern used in production pipelines.
  • Exceptions are routed, never swallowed: missing issue dates and expired IDs become a reviewer queue with evidence links, exactly the behaviour a compliance-support workflow needs.
  • The audit trail is the product: every action timestamped and reversible, so automated work stays defensible.

Demonstration 03

Two-way document reconciliation board

Financial automation is mostly about trust between two record sets. Here a synthetic bank statement and a matching ledger are reconciled line-by-line: matches confirm automatically, mismatches become reviewable exceptions with a controlled resolution path, and the audit trail records every decision. Nothing is swallowed; nothing is silently “fixed”.

reconciliation-board (synthetic records)

0/8 · statement

0/8 · ledger

What you are actually seeing

  • A two-way match: every statement line must find a ledger partner and vice versa, so one-sided matches become exceptions, not assumptions.
  • Exceptions keep their evidence: both sides, the variance and the reason code stay attached until a human disposes of them.
  • The run ends with a sealed audit summary, the output a finance team actually signs off.
